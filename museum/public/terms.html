Use information

Atlas Careers is personal software operated by Yinan Feng for an individual job search. The public website describes the project and professional experience. The private desktop workspace requires local authentication.

Application records

Employer postings and correspondence remain attributable to their sources. A listing, fit score or prepared resume is a research aid. Actual applications are recorded with submission evidence. The user reviews employer conditions, work permission, application declarations and decisions.

Google connection

The account owner authorizes Gmail read-only access and manages that authorization through Google and the local app. Data handling and local deletion controls are described in the privacy notice.
